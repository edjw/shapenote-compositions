Fi in the tenor. 

The V of V (it's a major II not the minor ii)
- 67b Dundee re fi do re/la
- 50 Cranbrook re fi re do
- 516 Oliphant re fi re la
- 480 Salineville re fi la re
- 469 Pilesgrove re fi do re

If fi in the tenor,
- bass: re
- tenor: fi
- alto: do/re/la
- treble: re/la/do

Then it's a full `sol si re` V chord afterwards



Exceptions
- 517 Rapture la fi do re
- 410b Kambia la fi do la
